# ADR 0001 — Independent Research Foundation

**Status:** Accepted design constraint, Sprint 0.

Agentic Trading is a completely independent project from Scalp City. Its research results and evidence must not rely on Scalp City synthetic records or shared deployment resources.

Sprint 0 implements a read-only local API and testable versioned contracts. Trading and student-creation operations remain disabled until their scheduled phases.

**Repository visibility:** Public at CEO request, 2026-10-25. Do not publish credentials, personal records or live account information.
