# UX — Three-tab navigation baseline

No fourth main tab; internal services are drill-downs. Mobile-first.

## Home
CEO oversight: scoped portfolio performance, training status, system health, approval requests, recent activity and emergency controls. Never mix historical, paper and live financial results.

## Students
List named students with individual status, current lesson, autonomy mode and research generation. Create Student chooses a unique name and either Autonomous or CEO-Directed learning. Markets are **skills**, not permanent student identities.

## Student Profile
Overview: AI progress briefing, learning direction and history.
Skills: Market expertise and trading skills, Skill Mastery Map and Mentor qualifications.
Memory: Good/Bad and neutral experiences, provenance and recall links.
Exams: Qualifying evidence, independent examiner reports, champion comparisons.
Progress: Strategy DNA and evolution history.

## Trading
Separate Historical / Paper / Live scope selector; scoped P&L/equity curve, positions, model versions, trade journal and Guardian decisions.

## Approvals
Exact student, model digest, strategy version, instrument, account, allocation, risk/leverage limits, expiry, supporting evidence and explicit approve/reject. Backend checks CEO identity rather than trusting the browser.
